Our Story

Farmers at the heart of P.S Agro Ltd.

We are building P.S Agro Ltd from the ground up as farmers and producers. Our focus is simple: grow and source good agricultural produce, process it responsibly, and deliver useful food ingredients to customers.

Our main focus is dehydration — helping preserve the value of onion and garlic beyond the harvest season while creating consistent ingredients for food businesses.
